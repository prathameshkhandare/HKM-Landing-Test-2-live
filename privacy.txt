1. Personal Information
We respect your privacy when you visit our website. You do not need to register or provide any personal information simply to access the general content of our website, including temple timings, programme details, event announcements and devotional resources.
However, we gather certain personally identifiable information under specific circumstances, such as when you make a donation, register for a programme, or submit an enquiry. We do not sell or trade such information to third parties. We do not share such information with third parties unless authorised by the person submitting the information or when required by law.
2. Donations
When you make a donation through our online donation page, we collect the following information:
Full name
Mobile number
Email address
Postal address and PIN code
We use this information to:
Acknowledge receipt of your donation and send you a confirmation
Despatch prasadam or seva-related items, where applicable
Issue an 80G tax exemption certificate upon your request
Communicate with you in case of any difficulty in processing the donation
If you are donating for a specific seva — such as Abhishekam, Annadanam, Go-seva, or any other temple programme — and wish the seva to be performed in a particular person’s name, we may additionally collect:
The sevakarta’s name and their relationship to you
Rashi, nakshatra and gotra — required to perform the sankalpa in the sevakarta’s name
3. Donor Personal Data
We retain your personal information collected at the time of your online donation for as long as it is required for statutory, legal or accounting purposes, including compliance with the Income Tax Act, 1961 (India).
We use your personal information only for the purposes for which it was collected. You may also opt to receive communications from us regarding temple festivals, events, special programmes, and volunteering opportunities, and we will use your personal data for these communications where you have given consent.
4. Programme and Course Registrations
We conduct various educational, devotional and cultural programmes for which online registration may be required. These include:
Gita Life Foundation Course — our online Bhagavad-gītā study programme
FOLK (Friends of Lord Krishna) — our youth empowerment programme
ICVK (Indian Culture and Values for Kids)
Vidyabhyasam — enriching communities with food, education and values
Other devotional programmes conducted at the temple
When you register online for any of these programmes, we collect information relevant to that specific programme, which may include:
Participant’s name, date of birth, gender and educational institution
Contact person’s name, relationship to the participant, email address, mobile number, city and postal address
Participant details are used to confirm eligibility, manage participation, communicate regarding the programme, and issue participation certificates or awards where applicable.
5. Yatramritam — Rediscover Pilgrimage
Yatramritam is our pilgrimage programme through which we organise spiritual yatras for devotees to sacred Vaishnava dhamas and pilgrimage destinations. To facilitate a smooth and safe pilgrimage experience, we collect personal information from all yatra participants at the time of registration.
When you register for a Yatramritam pilgrimage, we collect:
Full name, date of birth and gender
Mobile number and email address
Postal address and PIN code
Government-issued photo ID details (such as Aadhaar, PAN or Passport number) — required for travel bookings, hotel check-ins and entry to certain pilgrimage sites
Passport number and validity, where the yatra involves international travel
Emergency contact name, relationship and mobile number
Any dietary requirements, medical conditions or mobility needs that may affect your participation in the yatra
This information is used solely for the purpose of organising and facilitating the pilgrimage, including travel and accommodation bookings, sankalpa at dhama temples, coordination with local priests and service providers, and ensuring the safety and well-being of all participants. We do not share this information with any third party beyond what is strictly necessary to carry out the yatra arrangements.
6. Festival Seva Offerings
On the occasion of major Vaishnava festivals celebrated at Dakshina Dwaraka Dham — such as Janmāṣṭamī, Ekādaśī, Narasimha Jayantī, Rukmini Dvādaśī and others — devotees may donate for specific sevas performed on that day, such as Abhishekam, Annadanam, flower decoration, lamp offerings or other festival-related sevas.
When you donate for a festival seva, we collect:
Your name, mobile number and email address
Postal address and PIN code, where required for communication or receipts
The sevakarta’s name and their relationship to you, if the seva is to be offered in another person’s name
Rashi, nakshatra and gotra — required to perform the sankalpa in the sevakarta’s name
This information is used solely to acknowledge your donation, perform the sankalpa correctly, issue any applicable receipt or 80G certificate, and communicate with you regarding the seva.
7. Annadanam and Food Distribution Programmes
We operate daily Annadanam (free Prasadam distribution) at the temple, serving devotees, visitors and the needy. For individuals or organisations wishing to sponsor Annadanam or related food distribution programmes, we collect:
Sponsor’s name, mobile number and email address
Organisation name, if applicable
The preferred date and occasion for the sponsored meal
This information is used to acknowledge and confirm the sponsorship, coordinate the programme, and send any applicable receipts or certificates.
8. WhatsApp and Email Subscriptions
You may subscribe to receive communications from us via WhatsApp or email. These communications may include:
Daily deity darshana images
Festival and event announcements
Ekadashi reminders and devotional quotes
Gita Life course updates and other programme information
When you subscribe to our WhatsApp or email list, we collect your name and contact details (WhatsApp number or email address) for the sole purpose of sending you these communications. This service is absolutely free and you may unsubscribe at any time by contacting us at connect@hkmchennai.org or by using the unsubscribe option provided.
9. Enquiries (Contact Us)
When you send us an enquiry through the ‘Contact Us’ page on our website, we collect your name and email address along with your enquiry, in order to communicate with you and answer your questions. This information is not retained beyond the period necessary to respond to your enquiry.
10. Third-Party Tools and Services
Email Communication
We use an email service provider to communicate with our email subscribers. When you subscribe to our email updates, we collect your name and email address to send you news about upcoming festivals, special events and temple activities. You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe option provided in our emails.
Analytics
We use Google Analytics and/or Google Tag Manager to analyse and evaluate the use and performance of our website in order to improve your online experience. These tools collect non-personal data that may include your IP address, geographical location, browser type, operating system, referral source, length of visit, page views and website navigation paths.
This data is used in aggregate, anonymous form to understand how our website is used, determine the popularity of content, and measure the effectiveness of our communications. It is not used to identify you personally.
Payment Gateway
Online donations and payments on our website are processed through Razorpay, a secure third-party payment gateway. Razorpay may collect your contact details, card details and transaction details to facilitate your online payment. We do not collect or store any card or payment details, and we have no control over the data collected by Razorpay. Their data practices are governed by their own privacy policy.
11. Donation Refund Policy
We follow a reliable refund policy to ensure our donors feel confident in their association with us. We take the utmost care in processing donations as per the instructions provided by our donors.
In the unlikely event of an erroneous deduction, or if a donor wishes to cancel a donation, we will respond within 7 working days from the date of receiving the written complaint from the donor. The timely refund of any wrongly deducted amount will depend on the type of card or payment method used during the transaction.
To request a refund, the donor must provide:
Proof of deduction of the donation amount
A written communication requesting the refund, submitted within two days of the donation
If a donation receipt has already been issued, the donor must return the original receipt to our official address before the refund can be processed.
If an 80G tax exemption certificate has already been issued for the donation, a refund will not be possible.
International donations may require additional working days for processing.
12. Security
We are committed to ensuring that the information you share with us is kept secure. We have put in place appropriate physical, electronic and managerial procedures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, disclosure, unlawful processing, accidental loss, destruction or damage.
While we take all reasonable steps to protect your data, please note that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to our website.
13. Cookies
The third-party tools we use on our website may use cookies to automatically collect non-personal information about your visit. Cookies are small text files placed on your device by a website to recognise repeat visitors, facilitate your ongoing use of the site, and track usage behaviour to allow content improvements.
Cookies do not contain programs that damage files on your device. If you prefer not to have cookies collected during your visit, you can adjust your browser settings to deny or accept cookies. Please note that blocking cookies may affect the performance of certain features of our website.

Our website may contain links to other websites of interest, including affiliated ISKCON centres, devotional resources and partner organisations. The fact that we have provided a link to a site does not constitute an endorsement, sponsorship or affiliation with respect to that site, its contents, its owners or its providers, unless we expressly state otherwise.
We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external website you visit through links on our website.
15. Data Retention
We seek to retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by applicable law or regulation. For example, Indian tax laws may require us to retain the contact information and contribution levels of donors for a specified period.
When your information is no longer required, we will delete or anonymise it in a secure manner.
16. Your Rights
You have certain rights with respect to the personal information we hold about you:
Right to access — You may request details of the personal information we hold about you.
Right to rectification — You may request that we correct any incomplete or inaccurate information.
Right to restriction — You may request that we restrict the use of your information.
Right to erasure — You may request that we delete your information, unless we are otherwise required by law to retain it.
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at connect@hkmchennai.org. We will make reasonable efforts to respond to your request within a reasonable timeframe.
17. Sharing of Information
We make every good-faith effort to preserve your privacy. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.
We may disclose your personal information in the following limited circumstances:
When required by applicable law, regulation, court order or other legal process
When necessary to protect the rights, privacy, safety or property of HKM Chennai, our donors, volunteers or visitors
When necessary to enforce our terms of service or to investigate potential violations
In all other circumstances, your information will not be shared without your prior written consent.

If we make any material changes, we will post the updated policy on this page with a revised ‘Last Reviewed’ date, so that you are always aware of what information we collect, how we use it, and under what circumstances we may disclose it.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed of our current practices.
