5. Refund and Cancellation Policy
HKM Chennai follows a reliable and transparent refund policy to protect the interests of our donors and programme participants.
Donations
All donations are final once confirmed. Cancellation of a confirmed donation is not permitted. In the event of an erroneous or duplicate deduction, or if a donor believes a transaction was processed incorrectly, a refund request may be submitted within two working days of the transaction date.
To raise a refund request, the donor must provide:
Proof of the erroneous deduction (bank statement or payment gateway confirmation)
A written request for refund sent to connect@hkmchennai.org within two working days of the donation
If a donation receipt has already been issued, the original receipt must be returned to our registered address before the refund can be processed.
If an 80G tax exemption certificate has already been issued in respect of the donation, a refund will not be possible.
For transactions successfully debited by the payment gateway but not confirmed in our system, a refund will be processed after reconciliation, typically within 10–15 working days.
International transactions may require additional working days for processing.
Programme and Course Registrations
Registration fees paid for programmes such as the Gita Life Foundation Course, FOLK, ICVK, Vidyabhyasam or other devotional programmes are generally non-refundable once registration is confirmed. In exceptional circumstances, requests for refund or transfer of registration will be considered on a case-by-case basis and must be submitted in writing to connect@hkmchennai.org at least 5 working days before the commencement of the programme.
Yatramritam — Pilgrimage Yatras
Yatra bookings are confirmed upon receipt of the registration fee. Cancellation and refund policies for Yatramritam pilgrimages are as follows:
Cancellation more than 30 days before the yatra commencement date: refund of the registration fee less any non-recoverable costs already incurred (such as advance bookings for travel or accommodation)
Cancellation between 15 and 30 days before the commencement date: 50% refund of the registration fee, subject to deduction of non-recoverable costs
Cancellation less than 15 days before the commencement date: no refund
In the event that HKM Chennai cancels or postpones a yatra due to unforeseen circumstances, registered participants will be offered a full refund or the option to transfer their registration to a future yatra
All cancellation requests must be submitted in writing to connect@hkmchennai.org. Refunds, where applicable, will be processed to the original payment method within 10–15 working days of approval.
Festival Seva Offerings
Donations made for festival seva offerings — such as Abhishekam, Annadanam, flower decoration and lamp offerings on specific festival occasions — are non-refundable once the sankalpa has been performed and the seva has been offered to the Lord. Requests for cancellation made before the date of the festival will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

7. Temple Conduct and Visitor Guidelines
All visitors to Dakshina Dwaraka Dham, Thiruvanmiyur, are expected to observe the following standards of conduct in keeping with the sanctity of the temple:
Dress modestly and appropriately for a place of worship
Remove footwear before entering the temple premises
Maintain silence and reverence in the deity hall during arati and other sacred ceremonies
Refrain from photography or videography of the deities or inside the temple hall without prior permission from temple management
Abstain from consuming non-vegetarian food, alcohol or intoxicants on or near the temple premises
Follow the instructions of temple volunteers and management at all times
HKM Chennai reserves the right to ask any visitor or participant who does not observe these standards to leave the temple premises.
